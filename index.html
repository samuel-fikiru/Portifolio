MY Journey

With some earnings from part-time work, I began exploring software development, hacking, and related fields—mainly using just my phone. Despite the limitations, my passion pushed me to learn coding on my own. As I neared the end of high school, I took my journey more seriously, and now, three years into university, I’m still fueled by the same ambitions. Through self-study and my involvement in the computer science department, I’m continuously growing and striving to turn my dreams into reality.
